# Interview Guide

> Phase 3 artifact. Used during phase 4 interviews. Max 60 minutes.

- Recruited via:
- Date / interviewer:

## Warm-up (5 min)
- Tell me about your role and a typical week.
- When did you last deal with `<problem space>`?

## Past behavior (30 min) — the core
_Ask about the last concrete time, not hypotheticals._

1. Walk me through the last time you `<did X related to the problem>`. What triggered it?
2. What did you try? What happened next?
3. Where did it get frustrating or slow?
4. What did you do to work around it?
5. Who else was involved?
6. What would "good" have looked like?

## Workarounds & tools (10 min)
- What tools did you use? Why those?
- If those tools disappeared tomorrow, what would you do?

## Willingness signals (10 min)
- Have you ever paid to solve this? (Money, time, political capital.)
- Who inside your team cares most about this?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
- What question did I forget to ask?
- Can you connect me to 1 other person with this problem?

---

## Guardrails
- **Do not** pitch solutions during the interview.
- **Do not** ask "would you use X if we built it?" — hypotheticals are unreliable.
- **Do** ask "when did you last…" — past behavior predicts future behavior.
- **Do** shut up for 5 seconds after each answer; the second sentence is usually the real one.
